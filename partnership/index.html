How the partnership works

A clean application
reaches your desk.

AI never approves a transaction. It reads documents, checks completeness and flags risk, the bank always makes the final regulatory call.

01

Customer onboards digitally

The customer completes KYC, purpose selection and document upload through Money2Abroad's guided, mobile-first flow.

02

AI verifies and prepares

Documents are OCR-read and checked for completeness, expiry, name mismatches and purpose alignment before anything is submitted.

03

Bank reviews and approves

Your team receives a clean, pre-verified application, with risk notes and compliance checks already attached, and makes the final approval.

04

Bank settles and reports

The bank executes the FX, settles via SWIFT or partner rails, and completes RBI reporting, exactly as it does today.
